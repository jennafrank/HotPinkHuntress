About the Huntress

WHO IS HOT PINK HUNTRESS?

I'm a threat hunter and cybersecurity researcher with a specialty in adversary tracking, malware reverse engineering, and proactive defense. The hot pink is intentional — visibility in a sea of grey is a tactical choice.

My work sits at the intersection of threat intelligence and offensive security tradecraft. I analyze attacker techniques, map campaigns to MITRE ATT&CK frameworks, and convert raw intelligence into actionable detection logic defenders can actually use.

When I'm not hunting adversaries, I'm writing about the techniques I uncover — because open knowledge beats closed ecosystems. Every post is a weapon handed to the defensive community.
